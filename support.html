FND Sentinel Support

FND Sentinel helps a wearer and caregiver compare Apple Watch signals with locally logged episodes and warning signs.

Do not rely on an alert—or the absence of one—for safety. The app will miss events and can raise false alerts. Follow the care plan agreed with clinicians. If someone is unresponsive, injured, having trouble breathing, or may otherwise be in danger, contact local emergency services.

Requirements

  • An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
  • For watch features, a paired Apple Watch running watchOS 10 or later with the FND Sentinel watch app installed.
  • Apple Health permissions for whichever signals are available and you choose to share.

Getting started

  1. Open FND Sentinel on the iPhone and read the medical limitation during onboarding.
  2. Choose Allow and continue to review Apple Health and notification permissions, or skip and connect later from Settings.
  3. Wear the Apple Watch normally and keep the devices synced while the app builds a personal baseline.
  4. Log full episodes, near misses, and early warning signs consistently. Correct onset time is more useful than a long note.

Using the child-friendly watch screen

  • I feel funny saves an early warning marker.
  • Something happened asks whether it happened or almost happened, how strong it felt, and when.
  • Start check begins a live heart-rate check. It uses more battery and should be a deliberate, short-term action.

The watch status uses plain language and always tells the wearer to involve a grown-up when needed. It is pattern information, not a sure prediction.

The app says it is still learning

This is expected at first. Baselines need repeated Apple Health readings, and personal learning needs consistently logged episodes. More data does not turn the app into a reliable medical predictor; use Insights only as a retrospective sanity check.

Health or watch data is missing

  • Open iPhone Settings or the Health app and confirm FND Sentinel still has permission for the intended data types.
  • Confirm the Apple Watch is paired, unlocked, worn snugly, and has recently synced with the iPhone.
  • Open FND Sentinel on both devices, then pull down on the iPhone Now screen to refresh.
  • Some signals—including HRV, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, and wrist temperature—are measured intermittently and may not appear on every device or for every wearer.

Alerts

Risk alerts require sustained elevated readings and respect the configured quiet period. Sensitivity, confirmation count, quiet period, and watch haptics can be changed in Settings. Higher sensitivity means more alerts, not greater certainty.

Exporting for a clinician or caregiver

Open Settings → Export CSV for clinician. Review the generated files before choosing a recipient in Apple’s share sheet. Health and episode data is sensitive; share only with someone you trust.

Deleting data

Delete individual entries from Episodes using the standard list deletion gesture. Use Settings → Delete all data to remove all FND Sentinel records stored on the iPhone. Source data in Apple Health is not deleted.

Privacy

No account or developer server is used. Read the FND Sentinel Privacy Policy for the complete local-processing, WatchConnectivity, export, and deletion explanation. See Methodology and limitations for how the experimental personal pattern score works and where it can fail.
