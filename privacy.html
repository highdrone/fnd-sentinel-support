Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

FND Sentinel processes personal health signals and episode records locally on the wearer’s iPhone and paired Apple Watch. No account is required. The app sends William Evans no Health data, episode records, usage logs, analytics, or identifiers. The developer operates no app server, advertising, analytics, or tracking service.

Health data the app reads

With your permission, FND Sentinel reads heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, steps, sleep sessions, and resting heart rate from Apple Health. The watch can also read live heart rate during a check that the wearer starts. You control Health access in Apple’s Settings and Health apps.

Episode and derived pattern data

The app stores episode, near-miss, and warning-sign logs; optional symptoms and notes; personal baselines; derived feature snapshots and pattern scores; alert history; and app settings. These records are used on-device to compare recent signals with the wearer’s own prior patterns. They are not sent to the developer.

iPhone and Apple Watch communication

The paired devices exchange recent readings, logged events, settings, and pattern status through Apple’s WatchConnectivity system. FND Sentinel does not route that information through a developer-operated server.

Storage and security

App records are stored in the app’s private Application Support container with Apple complete file protection. The watch retains a small local status cache. Apple device backups and operating-system services are controlled by Apple and by your device and account settings.

Notifications

If allowed, FND Sentinel creates local notifications and watch haptics when its on-device pattern score crosses the configured threshold. Notification delivery is handled by Apple. The app does not send notification content to the developer.

User-directed export

The iPhone app can create a CSV export containing stored vitals, episode logs, pattern history, and alerts. Nothing is exported until a user selects this action and chooses a destination in Apple’s share sheet. Once shared, the selected recipient or service controls that copy under its own privacy practices.

Tracking, advertising, analytics, and usage logs

FND Sentinel includes no third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, developer-operated analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting SDK, or data broker integration. The app does not send the developer usage logs or identifiers. The developer does not receive, sell, or monetize app Health data, episode records, or app activity.

Children and caregivers

The watch interface may be used by a child with caregiver supervision. FND Sentinel does not knowingly transmit a child’s personal information to the developer. A parent or guardian should control device setup, Health permissions, exports, and any sharing of records.

Your controls and deletion

  • Change or revoke Health permissions in Apple’s Health or Settings app.
  • Turn risk alerts and watch haptics off in FND Sentinel Settings.
  • Delete individual episode records in the Episodes tab.
  • Use Settings → Delete all data to remove FND Sentinel’s locally stored records from the iPhone. This does not delete source records from Apple Health.
  • Deleting the apps removes their local containers, subject to Apple backup and restore behavior.

Medical limitation

FND Sentinel is not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, emergency service, or reliable predictor. Its outputs may be incomplete or wrong. Do not use the app for safety-critical decisions or instead of professional care.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before a release adds accounts, cloud sync, remote analytics, caregiver servers, advertising, tracking, or another material data practice. The effective date identifies the current version.
