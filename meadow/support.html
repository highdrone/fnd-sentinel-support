A little help getting started

Make yourself at home.

Choose a starting point, keep useful notes, and make your support plan easy to reach. The journal works with or without Health access and Apple Watch.

For Steady Meadow 3.0. Using an earlier build? Visit FND Sentinel support.

Set up your experience

  1. On the welcome screen, choose Make it mine. Pick the experience for yourself or a child you support, and add an optional name.
  2. Choose FND, Anxiety & worry, Panic support, Sensory overwhelm, or Everyday wellbeing. A template supplies prompts and optional ideas; selecting it is not a diagnosis.
  3. Begin with your journal. In solo mode, connect Apple Health if you want to include available measurements. For a child, authorize Health on the Watch itself; caregiver mode does not import the adult phone's Health history.
  4. To look around first, choose Explore with sample entries. The sample banner stays visible. Exit returns to your personal records; sample edits are temporary.

Open Settings from Today to review your setup. This is one wearer's journal. Switching modes or templates keeps existing entries and measurements; it does not create a second person's record. If older readings have an uncertain source or wearer, do not assume that changing the name assigns them correctly.

The phone and Watch work as a pair

The phone app requires iOS 17 or later and the Watch app requires watchOS 10 or later. Use an Apple Watch normally paired to the journal's iPhone. Apple Watch For Your Kids / Family Setup, a Watch paired to a different caregiver's phone, remote caregiver accounts, and multi-person records are not supported.

The child's Watch experience is friendly and simple; the solo experience uses quieter adult wording. Your selected mode, template, name, and saved support plan copy to the paired Watch after the devices communicate. The Watch retains that information for offline use. New phone changes do not appear on a disconnected Watch until it receives them.

Use the Watch without the phone nearby

  • I feel funny in child mode or Notice a change in solo mode saves a quick marker.
  • Swipe up for notes and tools. Something happened lets you record a little more about the moment. A successful save means it is stored on the Watch; it does not mean someone has read it.
  • Start recording begins a wearer-started live heart-rate check, after Health permission. Stop recording ends it. The workout-style session is discarded rather than saved to Fitness. Live recording uses battery and can be interrupted by watchOS, another workout, or power loss.
  • Open your saved support plan or use an optional pause tool. The character, landscape, and breathing animation do not represent a health or safety assessment.

Watch notes and available supported Health records can be saved locally while disconnected. This does not promise continuous sensor readings: availability depends on Apple hardware, region, permissions, measurement schedules, and watchOS.

When records are waiting to transfer

The Watch keeps pending records until the phone has saved a copy and acknowledged receipt. Interrupted transfers retry; stable record identifiers help avoid duplicates. The phone then imports its saved inbox into the journal. Apple's background scheduling controls when this happens.

  1. Keep both apps updated. Check that both devices have charge, free storage, and a working Bluetooth or Wi-Fi connection.
  2. Unlock the devices and open each app. Reopen the phone app if it was force-quit.
  3. Refresh Today or use Retry saved watch data in Settings if a delivery issue is shown.
  4. If a save error remains, retain the app and its data while you check available storage. Do not uninstall an app holding records you have not yet transferred or exported.

The phone does not have to remain open for every transfer, but Apple may delay background work. A pending record is not an emergency notification. Tell a trusted person directly when help is needed.

When Health readings are missing

Review permissions on the device collecting the readings. Finishing a permission screen does not prove the app can read every requested type. Some readings may never be available on your device or in your region. Denied permissions, ordinary measurement gaps, and missing data do not prevent you from using the journal or your support plan.

In caregiver mode, phone Health import is intentionally unavailable; use local Watch authorization. Revoking Health access does not erase records already copied into the app. The collector does not mirror later deletions from Apple Health.

Journal, Pause, and Care

Journal brings check-ins and logged moments together. You can edit or delete entries. Patterns summarizes recorded observations; a missing entry means unknown, not that no symptoms occurred. Measurements describe what was recorded and do not identify a cause.

Pause offers optional grounding, comfortable breathing, and a small next step. Choose only what feels useful. Stop or switch tools if one feels uncomfortable or makes things worse; there is no target you must reach.

In Care, write the steps agreed with your care team, what you prefer to avoid, emergency instructions, a trusted contact, and questions for your next visit. Support now displays your plan. The paired Watch receives your support steps, preferences, emergency instructions, and trusted-contact details for offline use. Appointment questions and phone journal notes stay on the phone and in any exports you choose to share. Contacts receive no automatic alerts or messages.

Share a useful summary

In Care, choose Preview visit summary. Read the complete report covering the previous 14 days, then choose Share this summary if you want to send it. CSV export is available in Settings. Creating a preview does not share it.

Exports may include sensitive journal and contact information. Your selected recipient or service controls its copy. The app requests exclusion of its records from device backups, so keep an export of anything you want to retain before replacing or erasing a device.

Delete data deliberately

To remove an individual entry, open it in Journal. To clear the phone record and pending inbox, use Settings → Delete this phone's app data. Apple Health, pending Watch records, and the Watch's cached setup and support plan are not erased by that phone action.

An offline Watch can still hold its last plan and queued records. Notes or readings may transfer later when it reconnects. To stop using a shared setup, also remove the Watch app and its local data. Removing the apps removes their local containers, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. See the Privacy Policy for the complete explanation.

Your care plan comes first. Steady Meadow does not detect or predict episodes, diagnose a condition, provide treatment, or send automatic health alerts. Do not assume new or unfamiliar symptoms are anxiety or panic. Seek medical advice for new or changing symptoms, and contact local emergency services when someone may be in immediate danger.
