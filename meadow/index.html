A personal journal, useful support tools, and a companion on your wrist. Make room for how things feel—and the moments that matter beyond them.

For myself

Your journal on iPhone. A quiet, practical companion on Apple Watch. Keep your own notes, support steps, and questions close.

For someone I care for

A friendly Watch experience for the child. A thoughtful journal and support plan for the parent or guardian on its paired iPhone.

Choose a starting point

Templates offer relevant prompts and optional support ideas. Choose what fits your experience; no diagnosis or label is required to use the journal.

Capture the moment. Return to your day.

Check in with energy and comfort, record a difficult moment, or save something worth remembering. Your Watch can save notes and gather supported local Health readings while the iPhone is unavailable, then offload them when the devices reconnect. Apple controls ordinary sampling and background delivery, so gaps and delays are possible.

Your support plan, within reach

Write what helps, what you prefer to avoid, and the steps agreed with your care team. Your paired Watch keeps a local copy for when your phone is away. Review a 14-day visit summary before choosing whether and where to share it.

One person's story, kept personal

No account, advertising, subscription, in-app purchase, or developer health-data server. The phone journal works without a Watch or Health permission. Records stay in local app storage, with paired-device transfers and exports you choose to share.

Both modes use one wearer's journal and a normally paired iPhone and Apple Watch. Changing modes does not create a separate record for another person. Family Setup, remote caregiver accounts, and emergency notifications are not supported.

Support, at your pace. Steady Meadow is a journaling and wellbeing support app, not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, episode detector, predictor, or emergency service. Choose only ideas that suit you and your care plan. Seek medical advice for new or changing symptoms, and emergency help when someone may be in danger.
